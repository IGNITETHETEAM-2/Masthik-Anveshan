BraTS Multi-Parametric MRI Laboratory

Four co-registered 3D MRI sequences (FLAIR, T1, T1-contrast enhanced, T2) capturing distinct biophysical tissue compartments for clinical tumor segmentation.

1. T1-Weighted Native (T1)

Structural Anatomy

Delineates normal anatomy; CSF appears dark (hypointense) while white matter appears bright. Useful for identifying non-enhancing necrotic cores.

2. T1 Contrast-Enhanced (T1ce)

Gadolinium DTPA

Highlights blood-brain barrier breakdown. Active neoplastic cells and hyper-vascular enhancing margins (ET) glow brightly.

3. T2-Weighted Spin Echo (T2)

Tissue Water & Swelling

Water and free fluid appear bright. Accurately reveals general cellular swelling and tumor margins against healthy parenchyma.

4. Fluid Attenuated Inversion Recovery (FLAIR)

Edema Delineation

Free fluid in ventricles is nulled (darkened), allowing peritumoral vasogenic edema (ED) to stand out with exceptional contrast.

Sub-Region Classification in BraTS Challenge

BraTS 2020 Multi-Channel

Necrotic / Non-Enhancing Core (NCR/NET)

Dead tissue center from rapid tumor outgrowth of blood supply. Hypointense on T1 and T1ce.

Enhancing Tumor (ET)

Actively dividing malignant tissue rim with disrupted blood-brain barrier. Avidly enhances on T1ce.

Peritumoral Edema (ED)

Vasogenic fluid infiltration into surrounding brain parenchyma. Exceptionally bright on T2-FLAIR.
